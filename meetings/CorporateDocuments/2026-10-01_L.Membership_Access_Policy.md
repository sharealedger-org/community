**Adopted:** October 1, 2026

---

## Authority

The bylaws of Sharealedger, NFP, Article 2a, authorize the Board of Directors to "determine
and set forth in a separate document the qualifications, dues, terms, and other conditions of
each class of member." This resolution constitutes that separate document for the initial
membership program.

---

## Resolution

The Board of Directors hereby adopts the following membership policy, effective immediately.

### 1. Membership mechanism

Membership in Sharealedger is established and maintained through access to the private
`sharealedger-org/members` GitHub repository ("the Members repository"). Access to the
Members repository constitutes membership. Removal of access constitutes termination of
membership.

The CEO grants membership by:

1. Adding the member to the `sharealedger-members` GitHub Team in the `sharealedger-org`
   GitHub organization, which provides read access to the Members repository and any
   other repositories the Board designates as member-access repositories;
2. Adding the member to the `members@sharealedger.org` member listserv; and
3. Recording the membership in the authoritative member register maintained in the
   private `sharealedger-org/admin` GitHub repository.

### 2. Membership classes

Per bylaws Article 2a, the Board hereby establishes two membership classes:

- **Individual Member.** Any person approved by the CEO. No dues are required at this
  time. Individual members are voting members per bylaws Article 2b.
- **Honorary Member.** Any person designated by the Board. No dues are required.
  Honorary members are non-voting per bylaws Article 2b.

The Board may establish dues requirements for either class by subsequent resolution.

### 3. Qualifications

There are no formal qualifications for Individual membership at this time beyond CEO
approval. The CEO may grant or deny membership at their discretion consistent with the
bylaws and the Antitrust Policy (Exhibit J).

Consistent with Exhibit J, membership shall be available on reasonable terms and shall
not be denied for anti-competitive purposes.

### 4. Application process

Prospective members may apply by:

- Filing a Membership Request issue in the public `sharealedger-org/community` GitHub
  repository; or
- Contacting the CEO directly at kip.twitchell@sharealedger.org.

The CEO will respond to requests within a reasonable time.

### 5. Copyright and licensing of member repository contents

Materials placed in the Members repository may carry individual copyright notices
specifying their own license terms. Such notices govern and supersede the Sharealedger
default Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license (Exhibit J) for those specific
materials.

A notice reading "© [Author], all rights reserved — circulated for private comment, not
for redistribution or citation without permission" means the material is not contributed
to Sharealedger and is not subject to CC 4.0. Members accessing such materials are
bound by the stated terms.

The placement of copyrighted materials in the Members repository by their author does
not constitute a contribution of those materials to Sharealedger for licensing purposes.
It constitutes use of Sharealedger's private infrastructure as a distribution mechanism,
with the author retaining all rights.

### 6. Member register

The CEO shall maintain an authoritative member register in the private
`sharealedger-org/admin` repository. The register shall record for each member: full
name, GitHub username, email, affiliation, membership class, date approved, approving
officer, and current status.

### 7. Resignation and removal

A member may resign at any time per bylaws Article 2l by notifying the CEO. Membership
is removed by revoking GitHub Team access and listserv access and updating the register.

The Board may remove a member per bylaws Article 2m.

### 8. Annual review

The CEO shall review the member register annually and confirm that GitHub Team access
and listserv membership are consistent with the register.

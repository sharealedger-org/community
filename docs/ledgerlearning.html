Delivered for Michigan State University students under the direction of Dr. William E. McCarthy, the inventor of the renowned Resource, Event, Agent (REA) economic accounting model. Bookkeeping and AI Series (Conversations with Tom Hall)

A 3-session in-depth dialogue with accountant and researcher Tom Hall, investigating mathematical and entity foundations needed for machine learning and AI to truly automate accounting tasks.

Session 1: The Entity & Questions

Tom explains the fundamental importance of an Entity and how historical accounting fails to capture modern questions.

Session 2: Metric Engines & Balances

Comparing search engines to metric engines, and preserving subledger keys without increasing cost.

Session 3: Automation Formulas

Impediments to AI adoption and going through Tom's mathematical variables and relationships.

Core LedgerLearning Themes

The Accounting Equation

Why Assets = Liabilities + Equity was revolutionary for manual books, and how modern databases can generalize dual-aspect accounting across multiple organizations.

The REA Framework

Resource-Event-Agent modeling pioneered by Dr. William E. McCarthy, separating the underlying economic events from the artificial debit/credit artifacts created to record them.

High-Performance Engines

Applying high-throughput data processing engines (like GenevaERS) to process millions of ledger balance updates in seconds rather than batch overnights.

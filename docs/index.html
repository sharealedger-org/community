An Organizational Home for Open Ledger Innovation

Sharealedger is an open, collaborative non-profit business league dedicated to improving business measurement, sharing financial data safely, and modernizing ledger architectures.

Explore LedgerLearning View Work & Exchanges

Core Principles

  • Consistent Data: High-performance ledger foundations with balance & transaction clarity.
  • Interoperable Standards: Multi-organization collaboration without proprietary vendor lock-in.
  • Accurate & Accessible: Bringing advanced financial management capabilities to 1 billion people worldwide.

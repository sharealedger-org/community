Membership & Participation

Join our collaborative community of researchers, practitioners, and engineers shaping the future of shared ledgers.

How Membership Works

Sharealedger operates a streamlined, repository-based membership model. Access to our private sharealedger-org/members GitHub repository constitutes active membership in Sharealedger, NFP.

Current Membership: 100% Free / Gratis

In accordance with our current board policy, all approved members are admitted gratis (no dues or fees required) during this active development and research phase.

Member Benefits & Downloadable Resources

Members receive direct access to our private working repository containing:

  • Research Monograph PDFs: Complete papers on the Minimum Cost Curve, Pre-Hierarchy Event Repositories, and the Master Accounting Framework.
  • Full Slide Deck Archives: Comprehensive PowerPoint presentation decks (e.g. CKB Spring Work Effort, Sharealedger Progression Decks, and MSU Lecture Materials).
  • Sanitized Real-World Data Fixtures: Multi-year sanitized transaction datasets and budget models for ledger testing and benchmark simulations.
  • Working Groups & Discussion: Participation in member working sessions and our community listserv (members@sharealedger.org).
  • Early Working Drafts: Advance access to pre-release whitepapers, engine specifications, and conceptual models.

How to Request Membership

  1. Submit an Issue: Open a Membership Request on GitHub using our standard template.
  2. Or Contact Directly: Email the Executive Director at kip.twitchell@sharealedger.org with your GitHub username and areas of interest.
  3. Invitation: Once reviewed, you will receive an invitation to the sharealedger-members GitHub team and be added to the member register and community listserv.

Member Expectations & Copyright

Members agree to adhere to our antitrust guidelines, code of conduct, and intellectual property terms:

  • Collaborative Spirit: Operating with an abundance mentality to share insights that advance the entire field.
  • Copyright Protection: Content in member repositories may carry individual contributor copyright notices. Members respect original author rights while contributing toward shared goals.
  • Antitrust Compliance: No discussion of pricing, customer allocation, or anti-competitive practices. Full policy available in Corporate Governance.

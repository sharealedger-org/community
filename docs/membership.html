Membership & Participation

Join our collaborative community of researchers, practitioners, and engineers shaping the future of shared ledgers.

How Membership Works

Under Board Resolution Exhibit L (Membership Access Policy), Sharealedger operates an agile, repository-based membership model. Access to the private sharealedger-org/members GitHub repository constitutes active membership in Sharealedger, NFP.

Current Dues Policy: Gratis

In accordance with the 2026 Board Policy, all current members are admitted gratis (no dues required) during this development and research phase. Future dues structures and member classes will be established by Board resolution when applicable.

Member Benefits & Resources

Active members receive:

  • Private Research Repository Access: Direct read and contribution access to sharealedger-org/members containing research papers, prototypes, and progression slide decks.
  • Full Slide Deck Archives: High-resolution presentation materials and complete slide progression decks.
  • Discussion & Working Groups: Participation in community working groups, technical forums, and the member listserv (members@sharealedger.org).
  • Early Working Drafts: Access to pre-release whitepapers and conceptual models.

How to Request Membership

  1. Submit an Issue: Open a Membership Request on GitHub using our standard template.
  2. Or Contact Directly: Email the Executive Director at kip.twitchell@sharealedger.org with your GitHub username and areas of interest.
  3. Invitation: Once reviewed, you will receive an invitation to the sharealedger-members GitHub team and be added to the member register and community listserv.

Member Expectations & Copyright

Members agree to adhere to our antitrust guidelines, code of conduct, and intellectual property terms:

  • Collaborative Spirit: Operating with an abundance mentality to share insights that advance the entire field.
  • Copyright Protection: Content in member repositories may carry individual contributor copyright notices. Members respect original author rights while contributing toward shared goals.
  • Antitrust Compliance: No discussion of pricing, customer allocation, or anti-competitive practices. Full policy available in Corporate Governance.

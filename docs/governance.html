Governance Overview

Sharealedger, NFP operates in full compliance with United States Internal Revenue Code Section 501(c)(6) guidelines for non-profit business leagues. All corporate actions, minutes, and exhibits are archived in our public community repository.

Corporate Bylaws (Exhibit A)

The bylaws establish the organizational purpose, board authority, membership classes (Individual, Business, Honorary), voting procedures, and fiscal stewardship.

Adopted October 1, 2026, Exhibit L establishes GitHub-based membership access, admitting current members gratis to the sharealedger-org/members private repository and establishing member register requirements.

Membership & Antitrust Policy (Exhibit J)

Sharealedger is committed to strict adherence to federal and state antitrust laws. Community members and meeting attendees must not discuss or exchange competitively sensitive information, including pricing, fee structures, customer allocations, or boycotts.

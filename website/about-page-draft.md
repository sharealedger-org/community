# About Sharealedger

## An organizational home for open ledger innovation

Sharealedger is an Arizona nonprofit corporation recognized by the Internal Revenue Service under Section 501(c)(6) as a business league. It provides an organizational home for open projects, shared knowledge, and community work focused on improving financial and other forms of performance measurement.

Sharealedger exists to promote innovation in ledger and business systems by:

- developing and sharing concepts for better bookkeeping and measurement;
- supporting open software and reusable business definitions;
- preserving knowledge that is often hidden inside established systems;
- encouraging collaboration among researchers, practitioners, implementers, and organizations; and
- helping useful ideas move toward practical adoption.

## Stewardship protects freedom

Open licenses give people meaningful freedoms to use, study, change, and share project work. Sharealedger's organizational role is to help preserve those freedoms over time.

The organization can provide continuity for project assets, licensing records, contributions, trademarks, and community decisions. Where appropriate and legally authorized, it can coordinate action to protect the rights granted by project licenses when those rights are infringed.

Executable code in Sharealedger projects is generally licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Business and conceptual materials are generally licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license. Individual works may include additional attribution, permission, or third-party rights notices.

## Current leadership and future participation

Sharealedger is currently led and operated by its founder and executive director, Kip Twitchell. Kip is presently the primary operating steward of the organization.

The organization is intended to grow beyond any one individual. Future participation may include board members, project stewards, working-group leads, contributors, members, and other community representatives.

## Projects and resources

Sharealedger is the organizational home for a family of related projects and resources. These include:

- conceptual work describing perspective-independent shared bookkeeping;
- software and prototype projects developed in community repositories;
- research and measurement work;
- LedgerLearning resources, whitepapers, lectures, and related educational material; and
- discussions about open, reusable financial and business-system foundations.

Each project may have its own scope, maintainers, implementation choices, and evidence. Sharealedger provides the broader organizational context without making every project a product or a settled standard.

## Governance

Sharealedger's governance documents include its bylaws, conflict-of-interest policy, whistleblower policy, document-retention policy, gift-acceptance policy, executive-compensation policy, joint-venture policy, membership and antitrust policy, privacy policy, and website terms of use.

Corporate documents, meeting records, project discussions, and current work are maintained through the Sharealedger community repository.

## Participate

Sharealedger welcomes thoughtful participation in its discussions, projects, and research. Contributions can take the form of questions, examples, technical work, business knowledge, review, documentation, or help making open work more useful to others.

Start with the current projects and discussions, review the applicable license and conduct expectations, and contribute where your experience can help move the work forward.
